# Direct Flash Style Definition

## Core visual identity

Direct Flash here means a photograph that plausibly feels lit by a flash close to the camera axis.

The style is built from five visible behaviors:

1. **Near-camera frontal illumination** — the near subject receives direct, immediate light rather than soft cinematic modeling.
2. **Depth-based separation** — the near subject reads brighter and more present than farther surroundings.
3. **Crisp highlight response** — skin sheen, glossy fabric, glass, metal, wet surfaces, tableware, and other reflective materials can show clear flash reflections.
4. **Restrained color** — neutral-cool overall tendency, reduced color clutter, no heavy blue wash.
5. **Candid realism** — raw, everyday, documentary energy; less polished than beauty lighting and less stylized than cinematic grading.
